The Uffizi Gallery

A focused route through one of Florence's great collections, looking at works, techniques, symbols and the people who commissioned them.

Make sense of an extraordinary collection

Florence's wealthy families were important collectors and patrons, commissioning works from artists who transformed European art. Many of the paintings associated with that history are now housed in the Uffizi Gallery.

The scale of the museum can be overwhelming without a clear route. Camilla helps you focus on key works and understand how they relate to one another, moving beyond names and dates to the stories behind the commissions and the world in which the paintings were created.

Her background as an art conservator adds another layer to the visit: materials, painting techniques and workshop practice can become part of the conversation, together with symbolism and patronage. The aim is to understand not only what a work represents, but also how and why it came into being.
