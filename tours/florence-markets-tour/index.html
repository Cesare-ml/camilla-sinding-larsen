Florence city and markets tour

Florence through two complementary stories: its monuments and the everyday food culture of the city.

History, streets and food traditions

This walk combines an introduction to Florence's historic centre with the atmosphere of a local market. It is a way to move between the monumental city and the everyday habits that continue to shape Florentine life.

Along the route Camilla introduces the history and architecture of Florence while also talking about ingredients, seasonal products and the traditions behind Tuscan cooking. The market gives the visit a more informal dimension and creates an opportunity to connect the city's past with how people live and eat today.

The itinerary can be adjusted according to the time available and the interests of the group, keeping the balance between city history and food culture flexible rather than following a fixed formula.
