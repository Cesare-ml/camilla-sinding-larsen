Palazzo Pitti and Boboli Garden

The Medici residence, its remarkable picture collection and one of Florence's most famous historic gardens.

From the Medici palace to the Boboli Garden

Cosimo I de' Medici acquired Palazzo Pitti in 1550 and transformed it into the new residence of the ruling family. The palace remained closely connected with the governors of Florence for more than three centuries before passing to the Italian state in the early twentieth century.

The visit moves through richly decorated rooms and the important collections assembled in the palace. Paintings by major artists, including Raphael and Artemisia Gentileschi, offer a way to explore not only the history of the Medici court but also the materials, techniques and artistic choices behind the works.

The tour continues into the Boboli Garden, where architecture, sculpture and landscape form an extension of the palace itself, with views opening back towards Florence.
