A day in the countryside

A slower encounter with Tuscany through the Mugello landscape, olive growing, walking routes and local food traditions.

An authentic countryside experience

This experience is centred on the Mugello area north of Florence and on the traditional relationship between farms, olive groves, woodland and historic routes.

Depending on the season and the day you want to create, the visit can include walks through chestnut woods, stories connected with Dante and the landscape, or historic Medici sites such as the area around Trebbio. The original experience also introduces the cultivation of olives and the production of local olive oil.

A countryside day can combine walking and cultural visits with local food and wine, creating a different rhythm from a museum or city tour. The details are arranged directly with Camilla according to the group, season and interests.
