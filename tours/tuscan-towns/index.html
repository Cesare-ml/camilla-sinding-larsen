Tuscany beyond Florence

Florence may be the region's best-known destination, but Tuscany is filled with historic cities, medieval hill towns and quieter places where art, architecture and local traditions can be experienced at a different pace.

Depending on your itinerary, a visit can focus on places such as San Gimignano and Volterra or on larger cities including Siena, Lucca and Pisa. Each offers a distinct artistic heritage, urban character and relationship with the surrounding countryside.

Camilla can help build a route around the places that fit your journey, linking monuments and history with local food, wine and the character of the landscape.
