Siena city tour

Medieval streets, Piazza del Campo, the Cathedral and the traditions that still give Siena its unmistakable identity.

A city with its own rhythm and traditions

Siena offers a very different experience from Florence. Its medieval street pattern, steep lanes and brick architecture create a compact city in which civic history and local identity are still immediately visible.

The walk can centre on Piazza del Campo, the civic heart of Siena, before continuing through the historic streets towards the Cathedral and other monuments chosen according to the time available and the interests of the group.

Along the way Camilla introduces the traditions and social history that have shaped Siena, helping connect its art and architecture with the living city rather than treating them as isolated monuments.

The itinerary is flexible and can be adapted for a first introduction to Siena or for visitors who want to spend more time on particular artistic and historical themes.
